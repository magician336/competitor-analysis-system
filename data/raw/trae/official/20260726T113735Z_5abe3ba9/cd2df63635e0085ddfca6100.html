Define Tasks. Review Results. AI Does the Rest.
TRAE Work breaks down your tasks automatically, calling on the right tools to get them done. You define what you need. TRAE Work executes. You review the final output. All project files live in a single Workspace. No more switching between apps, searching for the right version, or re-uploading documents. Everything stays in one place, and your workflow stays uninterrupted.
Broader Context In. More Intelligence Out.
TRAE Work reads and reasons across different types of context. Whether it's a .docx spec, a .csv dataset, a .pptx deck, or a Python script, TRAE Work understands them all and synthesizes information across your entire project to deliver structured, professional output. Preview results in real time as they're generated. Leave comments, request revisions, and iterate. Feedback and results live side by side.
Multi-Device. Parallel Execution.
Desktop and Web work seamlessly. Whether you're at your desk or on the move, your Workspace is always at your fingertips. Check progress, review results, pick up where you left off. Powered by the cloud, TRAE Work runs multiple tasks simultaneously in the background. No local resource constraints, no waiting for one task to finish before starting the next. Your work keeps moving, even when you step away.
